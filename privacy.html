Privacy policy

Last updated: May 2026 Effective immediately

GrowthFlow ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website, engage with our services, or communicate with us.

Section 01

Information we collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Personal information
Your name, phone number, email address, business name, and any other information you provide when contacting us or requesting our services.
Usage data
Information about how you interact with our website (e.g., pages visited, time spent, browser type).
Communication data
If you opt into SMS or email communication, we may store your preferences and communication history.
Section 02

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Deliver and improve our consulting and financial services.
  • Respond to inquiries and manage client relationships.
  • Send service updates, scheduling notifications, and optional promotional content (only with your consent).
  • Analyze usage to enhance our website and client experience.
  • Fulfill legal or regulatory obligations.
Section 03

SMS communication & consent

By opting in to receive text messages from GrowthFlow, you agree to receive messages related to our services, including appointment reminders, service updates, and optional offers.

  • Message frequency may vary depending on service engagement.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • You can opt out at any time by replying "STOP" to any message.
Section 04

Data security

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect your information. However, no online system is 100% secure, so we encourage users to be cautious when submitting personal information.

Section 05

Third-party sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share your information only with:

  • Trusted service providers who help us deliver our services (e.g., messaging platforms, scheduling tools).
  • Legal authorities if required to comply with applicable laws.
Section 06

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of marketing emails or texts at any time.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data.
  • Update your preferences or withdraw consent.
